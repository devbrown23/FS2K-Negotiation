# FS2K Negotiation: Call Closer

A phone negotiation training game with an RPG-style leveling system. Play realistic real estate calls and drills, earn XP, rank up from E to S, and build your stats.

## Play

Play it at the claude.ai link and sign in: progress saves to your account, works on any device, and puts you on the leaderboard. You can also open `index.html` in any browser to play as a guest, with progress saved on that device.

## How you level up

- **Stats:** Vocabulary, Handling, Rapport, Listening, Anchoring, Composure and Closing. Strong answers train them, and every level-up gives 3 points to assign. Each stat has an in-game effect, such as more time on the call timer or a better starting Trust score.
- **Ranks:** E (Lv 1), D (Lv 3), C (Lv 6), B (Lv 10), A (Lv 15), S (Lv 25).
- **Daily quest:** Clear a gate, get 5 vocab answers right and handle 3 objections, for bonus XP, stat points and a streak.
- **Titles:** Unlock titles such as Silver Tongue, Wall Breaker and S-Rank Negotiator.

## Training

- **Vocabulary Drill:** Swap weak words for strong ones ("contract" to "agreement", "but" to "and").
- **Objection Gauntlet:** Timed answers to common seller and buyer objections.
- **Mentor Trials:** Name the technique behind each line.

## Mentors' Library

You learn 23 techniques as you level up:

- **Chris Voss:** mirroring, labeling, calibrated questions, no-oriented questions, accusation audit, late-night FM DJ voice, "that's right", Ackerman bargaining, black swans and the rule of three.
- **Tom Ferry:** daily prospecting block, ask for the appointment, consult don't present, know your numbers, follow-up system and rehearsing scripts out loud.
- **Fisher & Ury:** interests not positions, objective criteria and BATNA.
- **Robert Cialdini:** reciprocity, commitment and consistency, and social proof.
- **Stuart Diamond:** trading things valued unequally.

Techniques are summarized in our own words from these authors' public work. This is a fan-made practice tool, not affiliated with or endorsed by any of them.
