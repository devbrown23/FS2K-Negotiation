# FS2K Negotiation: Call Closer

A phone negotiation training game you play in the browser. Work through realistic real estate calls, earn XP, level up, and build five skills: Rapport, Listening, Anchoring, Composure and Closing.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any browser. It needs no build step and no server. Progress is saved in your browser.

## Calls

| Level | Call | Situation |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Lowball Offer | A buyer's agent comes in $45k under list |
| 1 | Commission Pushback | A seller wants you to match a 4% agent |
| 2 | Inspection Repair Demands | Buyers want an $18k roof credit |
| 3 | The FSBO Cold Call | Book a meeting with a for-sale-by-owner seller |
| 4 | Six Offers on the Table | Get your buyers' offer chosen |
| 5 | The Low Appraisal | Save a deal that appraised $15k low |
